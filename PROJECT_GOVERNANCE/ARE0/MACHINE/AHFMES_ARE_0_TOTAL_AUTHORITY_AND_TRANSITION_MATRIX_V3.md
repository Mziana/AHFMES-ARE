## 1. R9-04 — unified Champion revalidation state machine

### 1.1 Historical proof versus mutable current reliance

Historical `ProofBundle`, `PROOF_RELIANCE_ROOT`, and historical selection reliance remain immutable as-of evidence.

For each Champion selection generation, `ChampionRelianceRegistry` is a versioned current-reliance record:

```text
RELIANCE_STATE = CURRENT | SUSPENDED | REVOKED
RELIANCE_REVISION = monotone revision under exact predecessor CAS
```

Successful Promotion atomically creates revision 0:

```text
ChampionRelianceRegistry[Champion selection generation, revision 0]
= CURRENT
```

and binds the exact historical proof reliance, selection reliance and pre-outcome `CHAMPION_LIFECYCLE_POLICY_BUNDLE_ROOT`.

`REVOKED` is sticky for revalidation. Revalidation can never move `REVOKED` to `SUSPENDED` or `CURRENT`.

### 1.3 Immutable-at-birth opportunity class and key

Revalidation opportunity class is an immutable-at-birth derived value:


Generation rule:

An already-born opportunity is never reclassified after its result is known.

```text

no unresolved conflicting reconstruction remains
```
